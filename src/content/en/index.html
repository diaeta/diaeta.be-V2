Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Common questions from Brussels patients about our dietitian services

Do I need a doctor's referral to see a dietitian?

No referral required! In Belgium, you can book a dietitian appointment directly without a doctor's referral. However, we often work collaboratively with your GP or specialist when beneficial.

If you have a medical prescription or recent lab results, please bring them along—they help us provide more targeted care.

Are consultations reimbursed by insurance?

Partial reimbursement is often available. Many Belgian mutual health funds (mutuelles) offer reimbursement for dietitian consultations, typically ranging from €10-25 per session.

We provide compliant receipts and attestations. Check with your specific mutual fund for exact coverage details and any required documentation.

What qualifications do your dietitians have?

Fully accredited professionals. Our dietitians are:

  • INAMI/RIZIV registered (official Belgian healthcare registration)
  • Monash University FODMAP certified
  • Continuously educated in latest nutrition science
  • Experienced in clinical and practical nutrition
Which conditions do you specialize in?

Four core specialties:

  • IBS & Low-FODMAP: Monash-certified approach to digestive health
  • Weight Management: Sustainable, science-based weight loss
  • Type 2 Diabetes: Blood sugar control and meal planning
  • Cholesterol & Hypertension: Heart-healthy nutrition strategies

All programs are personalized and evidence-based.

What is nutrigenetic testing and is it worth it?

Personalized nutrition based on your DNA. Nutrigenomix® testing analyzes 70+ genetic markers to tailor recommendations to your unique biology.

Benefits include: Optimized macronutrient ratios, personalized vitamin needs, caffeine sensitivity insights, and performance optimization.

At €275, it's an optional add-on that can significantly enhance your nutrition plan's precision. We'll help you decide if it's right for your goals.

Do you offer family or couple consultations?

Yes, we love working with families! We offer special couple consultations at €130 (instead of €160) for first appointments.

Family sessions help ensure everyone is aligned on healthy eating goals and can be particularly effective for:

  • Household meal planning
  • Children's nutrition education
  • Supporting family members with specific conditions
How long are consultations and what's included?

Comprehensive, time-appropriate sessions:

  • First consultation: 60 minutes - Complete assessment, body composition, goal setting
  • First follow-up: 40 minutes - Progress review, plan adjustments
  • Regular follow-ups: 30 minutes - Monitoring, support, fine-tuning

Every session includes personalized recommendations you can implement immediately.

Do you offer video consultations?

Yes, secure video consultations are available! They provide the same quality of care as in-person visits with added convenience.

Benefits: Flexible scheduling, no travel time, same professional support, and easy follow-up access.

Available during daytime and evening hours. Book directly through our online booking system.

What is your cancellation policy?

24-hour notice required. Please notify us at least 24 hours in advance to cancel or reschedule your appointment.

Late cancellations (less than 24 hours) may incur a fee to cover the reserved time slot. This policy helps us maintain availability for all patients.

Emergency situations are handled with understanding—just contact us as soon as possible.

Do you provide mobile app support?

Yes, we use a comprehensive mobile app! It helps you track progress and stay connected between appointments.

Features include:

  • Food diary logging with photo uploads
  • Progress tracking and goal monitoring
  • Direct messaging with your dietitian
  • Meal planning and recipe suggestions
  • Appointment reminders and scheduling
How often should I have follow-up appointments?

Frequency depends on your goals and progress. Typical patterns include:

  • First month: Weekly or bi-weekly for initial support
  • Months 2-3: Every 2-3 weeks for adjustment and motivation
  • Ongoing: Monthly or as needed for maintenance

We'll work together to find the right rhythm for your lifestyle and goals.

What payment methods do you accept?

Flexible payment options:

  • Cash payments
  • Bancontact via QR code
  • Bank transfer (for advance payments)

Payment is typically made at the end of each consultation. We provide receipts for insurance reimbursement.

What makes your IBS/FODMAP approach different?

Monash University certified expertise. We follow the gold-standard Low-FODMAP protocol with a personalized approach.

Our method:

  • Step-by-step elimination with detailed tracking
  • Systematic reintroduction to find YOUR tolerances
  • Meal systems that fit your daily life
  • Ongoing support as symptoms change

We don't just give you lists—we help you understand your unique triggers.

How do you approach sustainable weight loss?

No diets, no restrictions—just sustainable change. Our approach focuses on:

  • Satiety-based eating: Foods that keep you satisfied longer
  • Body composition goals: Not just weight, but healthy muscle and fat ratios
  • Social life integration: Strategies for dining out and special occasions
  • Relapse prevention: Building habits that last beyond the program

Average success rate: 92% with personalized pacing.

Our approach includes:

  • CGM-aware coaching when available
  • Restaurant and travel meal planning

We coordinate with your doctor to ensure all recommendations support your medical treatment plan.
